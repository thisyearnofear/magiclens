What it gates

The MagicLens backend packs promotion evidence into 14 bits — minted status, playable media, leaderboard rank, quantized votes — and calls eval() on the processor. If the circuit rejects or the call fails, promotion fails closed: no Iconic Moment is minted to Flow. Verdicts are journaled and surfaced in the product ("Cleared by TapeOut #20 · score N/15" in the Screening Room).

Honest scope: the circuit verifies the packed policy bits, not raw vote counts or media authenticity — a NAND netlist cannot hash arbitrary input. That boundary is stated in the product UI.

Verify it yourself

eval() is permissionless and read-only. Live status: api/tapeout/status · in-browser re-verification: Screening Room → "Live Verify" (eth_call, no wallet needed).
